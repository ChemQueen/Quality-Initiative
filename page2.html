Getting Started with the Ecological Validation Model

Recommendations

  1. Train institutional agents on validation and how to implement validation in practice.
  2. Identify existing programs and offices to examine how and where validation occurs.
  3. Create a culture in which all interationcs with students strive to be validating experiences.

Questions to Guide Practice

  • What types of training or development might you need to utilize validation in your own work? To support others in using validation?
  • What are the assets, strengths, and capabilities of the at-promise populations at your institution?
  • What validating practices does your institution, program, or course already use? What has been successful? Challenging?
